🔬 Project Overview

This repository serves as a technical showcase of my research at Cornell University, focusing on large-scale data architecture and numerical modifications within the CESM GCM framework. The core objective is to simulate the spectral and bolometric impacts of space-based solar radiation management (SRM) strategies.

🖥️ HPC Infrastructure: All simulations and data pipelines were architected and executed on the NCAR Derecho High-Performance Computing (HPC) cluster, utilizing NetCDF4 standards for high-throughput I/O of 60 TB+ datasets.

📁 Data Standards: Why NetCDF?

The projects in this repository utilize NetCDF (Network Common Data Form), the industry standard for array-oriented scientific data:

  • Multidimensional: Storage of variables (temperature, pressure, solar irradiance) across 4D space-time coordinates ($x, y, z, t$).
  • Self-Describing: Metadata ensuring data integrity during high-throughput I/O.
  • Performance: Optimized for large-scale parallel reads/writes on HPC clusters like NCAR Derecho, managing 60 TB datasets.

🚀 GCM Numerical Implementations

1. Bolometric Dimming and Climatic Response

  • Framework: CESM GCM with the Community Atmospheric Model (CAM).
  • Implementation: Hardcoded parameterization of uniform, non-spectral solar constant reductions (bolometric dimming).
  • Analytics Workflow: Statistical analysis of bolometric forcing on stratospheric/surface anomalies and Arctic sea ice preservation.
  • Data Architecture: Optimized low-level Fortran I/O pipeline to ingest NetCDF4 parameters directly into the GCM core.

2. Filter Analysis, Spectral Dimming and Climatic Response

  • Framework: CESM with the Whole Atmosphere Community Climate Model (WACCM).
  • Implementation: Numerical integration of a space-based (L1) spectral filter into the GCM radiative transfer core.
  • Analytics Workflow: Evaluation of wavelength band radiation attenuations on the near infrared. Computed multidimensional diagnostics under the SSP2-4.5 future scenario.
  • Big Data Scale: High-throughput I/O for 60 TB climate datasets via parallelized Python (Xarray/NumPy) workflows.

3. 4D Spatio-temporal Matrix Implementation

  • Framework: Modification of solar forcing arrays inside CESM GCM radiative transfer and chemical modules.
  • Implementation: Architected and embedded 4D numerical matrices to simulate solar forcing dimming variability in time, latitude, longitude, and wavelength.
  • Analytics Workflow: Analysis of multi-variable climate responses to localized radiative forcing over a 30-year timeline [2035, 2065].
  • Core Skills: Advanced Fortran modifications, multidimensional coordinate mapping, and high-resolution 3D visualization.
