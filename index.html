High-Performance Computing & Climate Data Analytics Showcase

Technical summary: Multi-terabyte architecture & Computational Physics

🔬 Project Overview

This repository serves as a technical showcase of my research at Cornell University, focusing on large-scale data architecture and numerical modifications within the Community Earth System Model (CESM GCM) framework.

🖥️ HPC Infrastructure: All simulations and data pipelines were architected and executed on the NCAR Derecho HPC cluster, utilizing NetCDF4 standards for high-throughput I/O of 60 TB+ datasets.

🚀 GCM Numerical Implementations

1. Bolometric Dimming and Climatic Response

Hardcoded parameterization of non-spectral solar constant reductions for baseline climate sensitivity testing. Optimized the low-level Fortran I/O pipeline to ingest NetCDF4 parameters directly into the GCM core.

2. Filter Analysis and Spectral Dimming

Numerical integration of a space-based (L1) spectral filter into the WACCM radiative transfer core. Developed spectral analysis pipelines for wavelength band radiation attenuations under the SSP2-4.5 scenario.

3. 4D Spatio-temporal Matrix Implementation

Architected and embedded 4D numerical matrices into the model's core to simulate solar forcing variability in time, latitude, longitude, and wavelength over a 30-year timeline [2035, 2065].

📁 Data Standards: Why NetCDF?

The industry standard for array-oriented scientific data:

  • Multidimensional: Storage of variables across 4D space-time coordinates ($x, y, z, t$).
  • Self-Describing: Metadata ensuring data integrity during high-throughput I/O.
  • Performance: Optimized for parallel reads/writes on NCAR Derecho.
